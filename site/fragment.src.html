How it works

Three things. The third one happens by itself.

01 · CLIP

Cut the moment

Any Twitch or Kick stream, any tool. Put the CUT watermark on the clip from second three, never on the first frame. That is the only branding rule.

watermark ≥ 3s · 1080p · no other logos
02 · POST

Post it on X

Your account, your caption, your audience. Register the account once with the wallet that should get paid. The network's own accounts post the same way, no special lane.

one wallet per account · verified accounts first
03 · PAID

Get paid on Sunday

Views on every watermarked clip are counted. The more views, the bigger your share. You get paid in CUT on Robinhood Chain, one claim, every week.

every Sunday 20:00 UTC · every payout on the explorer

For clippers

Anyone with a clip account can join. The rules fit on one screen.

  • 01Watermark from second three. CUT bottom right, at least 3 seconds in. Clips with the tag on the first frame do not count; X treats the first frame as the poster.
  • 02Real views only. Minimum 10,000 views per clip to be counted, a cap per account per week, and views are read from X analytics, not screenshots.
  • 03One wallet per account. Register once. The wallet is on Robinhood Chain and it is the only place the payout goes.
  • 04Verified accounts first. The first weeks pay the network's own accounts and clippers we verify by hand. After that, registration is open to anyone who clears the view minimum.
  • 05No shilling in the caption. The clip is the product. The ticker lives in the watermark, not in the text. Captions that read like ads are excluded from the week.
  • 06Sunday, 20:00 UTC. Views are counted, the table is published here, and payouts go out the same hour.
